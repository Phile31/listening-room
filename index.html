01 / The room

Built for listening.

A custom 21-by-15-foot space with 10-foot walls and a 16-foot vaulted ceiling, totaling roughly 4,100 cubic feet.

The shell uses double-thick drywall over Rockwool and mass-loaded vinyl. The hardwood floor sits over a concrete slab, plywood, and rubber; a 10.5-by-17.5-foot wool rug rests on a thick felt pad.

Acoustic treatment includes two eight-foot range-limited soffit bass traps, two skyline diffusers, and a dozen wall- and ceiling-mounted absorption panels. Power comes from a dedicated line with two 20-amp outlets and 12-gauge wiring to the panel.

04 / Notes on the gear

What’s mine, what isn’t

Roughly 60 percent of the components and speakers listed here are my personal reference gear, purchased with my own funds. Others are on extended loan or in for review and remain the property of their manufacturers.

I do not accept financial or in-kind compensation from manufacturers, importers, dealers, or other stakeholders. Continued use of borrowed equipment does require ongoing relationships with the companies and people involved, so I know some reviews could be subject to reciprocity bias. I guard against this with the same confidence a man has that he doesn’t snore.

Once or twice a year, I’m invited on a factory tour whose expenses may be borne, in whole or in part, by my hosts. There are no quid pro quos. I make it clear—pleasantly and appreciatively—that I don’t guarantee coverage of any kind. Yes, I’ve visited companies I never subsequently wrote about.
